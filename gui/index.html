midi2g2

Drop a MIDI file, an NSF / NSFe file or a SID file (Commodore 64 music), and get a Nord Modular G2 performance that plays the whole piece, melodies and chords, from the G2’s own sequencers, synced to the master clock.

Drop a MIDI, NSF or SID file here or click to choose one (.mid, .midi, .nsf / .nsfe, or .sid for Commodore 64 music)
Slots
Slot D plays nothing itself: it transposes everything from the keyboard and adds reverb.
A coarser grid fits a longer piece in one performance. The G2 always runs at the piece’s real tempo.
The G2 sequencers hold whole semitones. “Main pitch” gives each note the pitch it holds longest, so scoops and vibrato don’t turn into stepped slides.
Sounds Every slot carries all 8 as variations: the one picked here is where it starts.

Section

Drag the two handles, or type bar numbers. Only this section is converted and played by “Selected parts” and “Play slots”.

A melody slot holds up to 31 bars of 16 steps: its sequencers and its synth share the voice area. A chord slot plays up to 4 notes at once, each note on its own oscillator straight from its sequencer, with chord changes on a slower step (a beat, two beats or a bar), so it covers far more time per sequencer. Longer pieces come out as several numbered performances. Only convert music you have the rights to use or share.
